## Severity guide
- **Critical:** Site or essential workflow unavailable; severe data/security impact.
- **High:** Major feature broken with no reasonable workaround.
- **Medium:** Feature partially works or significant usability impact.
- **Low:** Cosmetic, content or minor usability issue.

## Priority guide
- **P0:** Immediate fix required.
- **P1:** Fix in the current release.
- **P2:** Fix in a planned iteration.
- **P3:** Minor enhancement / backlog.
